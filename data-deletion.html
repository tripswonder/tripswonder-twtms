User Data Deletion

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This page explains how users can request deletion of personal data associated with their Trips Wonder account, including data created through Facebook Login, Google Sign-In, or email/password authentication.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Contact Trips Wonder Travel & Tours through our official website or official customer support channel.
  2. State that you are requesting deletion of your Trips Wonder account and personal data.
  3. Provide the email address associated with your Trips Wonder account so we can identify the correct account.
  4. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request to protect your account.

What We May Delete

Subject to applicable legal and business requirements, deletion may include:

  • Trips Wonder customer profile information
  • Authentication-linked profile information stored by Trips Wonder
  • Saved preferences and account settings
  • Profile photo references associated with the account
  • Messages and other customer account data where deletion is appropriate

Information We May Need to Retain

Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution, booking records, or legitimate business purposes.

Facebook Login

If you used Facebook Login, you may also remove Trips Wonder from your Facebook account's connected apps or permissions. Removing the Facebook connection does not automatically guarantee deletion of all data already stored by Trips Wonder. To request deletion of Trips Wonder-held data, follow the instructions on this page.

Important: Trips Wonder does not receive or store your Facebook password or Google password. Authentication is handled by the selected sign-in provider and Firebase Authentication.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Trips Wonder handles personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.
