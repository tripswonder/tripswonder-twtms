Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Trips Wonder Travel & Tours ("Trips Wonder", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use our website, customer account features, booking services, and sign-in options, and how we use and protect that information.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Trips Wonder, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo, when provided by your sign-in provider
  • Account identifier and authentication information
  • Booking and trip-related information you submit to us
  • Messages, preferences, and account activity within Trips Wonder
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure our website

2. Sign-In Providers

Trips Wonder may allow you to sign in using email and password, Google, or Facebook. When you choose a third-party sign-in provider, that provider may send us basic account information that you have authorized, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and provider-specific user ID.

We do not receive or store your Google or Facebook password.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Trips Wonder account
  • Authenticate your identity when you sign in
  • Manage bookings, trips, payments, and customer requests
  • Provide account notifications and travel-related updates
  • Enable customer support and messaging features
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

4. Firebase and Service Providers

Trips Wonder uses technology and service providers, including Google Firebase, to support authentication, account data, database functionality, hosting, security, and related website services.

These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own terms and privacy requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, process bookings, provide customer support, comply with law, protect our rights or users, or work with service providers that support Trips Wonder.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including account management, booking records, customer support, security, legal compliance, and legitimate business requirements.

7. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Trips Wonder account and associated personal information, subject to information that we may need to retain for legal, security, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

Our user data deletion instructions are available at: Trips Wonder Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Trips Wonder services are intended for users who are legally able to make or participate in travel arrangements. If information about a minor is provided for a booking, it should be submitted by a parent, guardian, or other authorized adult where required.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Trips Wonder is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we may update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or a privacy request, please contact Trips Wonder Travel & Tours through our official website or official customer support channels.
